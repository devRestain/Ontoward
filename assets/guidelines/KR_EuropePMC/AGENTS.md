# Korean-Source Europe PMC Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `europepmc_*.md` as converted open-access articles selected into the Korean-source Europe PMC group.
- Distinguish clinical guidelines, recommendations, and consensus papers by the catalog's document type.
- Read the document language metadata because this group includes both English and Korean articles.

## Retrieval Use

- Use relevant society guidance and article sections for cited clinical evidence retrieval.
- Distinguish article publishers and authoring societies from Europe PMC as the hosting source.
- Keep research and reporting guidance separate from patient-care recommendations when interpreting a retrieved article.

## Source Handling

- Inspect each document's Creative Commons variant rather than assuming a shared reuse condition.
- Compare article identifiers, titles, and source URLs when overlapping material appears in other source groups.
