# KDCA Public Health Information Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `health_kdca_*.md` as converted disease, examination, prevention, and health-management pages from KDCA's health-information portal.
- Treat this group's catalog role as general-public information rather than clinician guideline authority.
- Read the language field for each document instead of inferring it from the KR prefix.

## Retrieval Use

- Use these pages for patient-facing explanations, terminology, and contextual health information.
- Separate patient education content from clinician recommendation strength and diagnostic evidence.
- Preserve the page's overview, test purpose, and management context when selecting passages.

## Source Handling

- Preserve the recorded Korea Open Government License Type 4 condition for each source reference.
- Keep locally generated education wording or summaries separate from redistributed source modifications.
