# KDCA Manuals and Guidelines Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `kdca_*.md` as converted KDCA guidance and manuals from PDF, HWP, and HWPX source attachments.
- Distinguish infection-control, prevention, chronic-disease counseling, and institution-facing guidance by document context.
- Use titles and catalog types to distinguish a clinical recommendation from administrative or public-health procedure.

## Retrieval Use

- Retrieve sections matching the disease, population, institutional setting, and source year.
- Keep administrative reporting or facility instructions separate from individual patient-care decisions.
- Inspect heading and table conversion before using text from long manuals.

## Source Handling

- Preserve each document's recorded Korea Open Government License type instead of assigning one type to the whole group.
- Retain source attachment identity and URL when preparing cited retrieval.
