# KoMGI Guideline and Patient Materials Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `komgi_*.md` as converted materials from the Korean Medical Guideline Information Center.
- Distinguish clinical guidelines from summaries, patient Q&A, and prevention or management leaflets.
- Read publisher and language metadata rather than assigning the portal as every document's author.

## Retrieval Use

- Use clinician documents for guideline evidence and patient materials for explanation context.
- Preserve attachment identity when one portal entry supplies multiple documents.
- Check the document audience and full-title context before ranking a retrieved passage.

## Source Handling

- Retain the source's recorded Creative Commons or Korea Open Government License condition.
- Keep unspecified license-type records explicit instead of promoting them to unrestricted reuse.
