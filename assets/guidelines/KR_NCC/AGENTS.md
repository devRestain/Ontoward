# Korean National Cancer Center Materials Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `ncc_*.md` as converted National Cancer Center screening-quality guidance, result-action materials, and cancer-related patient information.
- Distinguish screening protocols and institutional result handling from patient-facing supportive-care material.
- Use the catalog and source URL to identify the original attachment and intended audience.

## Retrieval Use

- Use relevant screening and follow-up sections as cited context for separately implemented planning.
- Keep screening recommendations separate from diagnosis of an already symptomatic patient.
- Inspect sparse headings, infographic text, and converted tables before relying on a passage.

## Source Handling

- Preserve each document's recorded Korea Open Government License Type 4 metadata.
- Retain attachment-specific provenance for preparation outputs.
