# NECA Evidence and Guidance Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `neca_*.md` as converted Korean National Evidence-based Healthcare Collaborating Agency reports and guidance.
- Distinguish guideline-development research, recommendations, summaries, and patient-facing material.
- Read document metadata because this group includes Korean and English sources.

## Retrieval Use

- Use research methods and evidence sections to understand how a recommendation was developed.
- Separate a guideline-development study from a finalized clinical recommendation.
- Inspect long-report structure and conversion limits before selecting recommendation text.

## Source Handling

- Retain the document's specific license metadata, including differing Korea Open Government License and Creative Commons labels.
- Preserve report and attachment identifiers in data-owned provenance.
