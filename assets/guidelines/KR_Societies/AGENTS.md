# Korean Medical Society Guidance Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `kr_society_*.md` as PDF-derived clinical guidelines, recommendations, and consensus material distributed by Korean medical societies.
- Use the organization field to identify the issuing society for each document.
- Read language metadata because society-distributed material includes Korean and English text.

## Retrieval Use

- Retrieve the document's actual recommendation section rather than relying on its download-page title.
- Preserve society, revision year, clinical population, and recommendation context.
- Check source identity against journal-hosted versions before treating duplicate text as independent evidence.

## Source Handling

- Inspect each source's Creative Commons or society-permission condition.
- Preserve the noncommercial and no-adaptation wording attached to society-permission records.
