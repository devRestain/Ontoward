# CDC and ACIP Guidance Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `cdc_*.md` as converted CDC and ACIP recommendations and guidance, including MMWR-derived material.
- Distinguish vaccination, infection prevention, treatment, and outbreak-specific guidance through the document body.
- Treat cataloged public-domain labels as source metadata to retain with citations.

## Retrieval Use

- Match vaccine, population, infection, outbreak setting, and publication date before using recommendations.
- Separate routine recommendations from outbreak-specific or interim guidance.
- Keep agency and committee attribution attached to the selected passage.

## Source Handling

- Preserve conversion context for tables, schedules, and qualification statements.
- Avoid presenting a captured historical or interim document as verified current guidance.
