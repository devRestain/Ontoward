# US-Grouped Europe PMC Articles Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `europepmc_*.md` as converted articles selected into the US-grouped Europe PMC collection.
- Distinguish guidelines, recommendations, and consensus articles by document type and actual subject.
- Read authoring organization and population metadata rather than inferring US-only authorship or applicability.

## Retrieval Use

- Use clinically relevant article sections for cited evidence retrieval.
- Distinguish patient-care guidance from research-method, reporting, or commentary recommendations.
- Compare PMC identifiers and source URLs when overlapping publications appear elsewhere.

## Source Handling

- Inspect the full document-specific Creative Commons or public-domain label.
- Retain article limitations and recommendation context when preparing search passages.
