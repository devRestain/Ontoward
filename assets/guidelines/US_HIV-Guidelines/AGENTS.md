# HHS HIV Guidelines Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `hiv_dhhs_*.md` as full PDF-derived HHS HIV guideline documents.
- Distinguish adult and adolescent antiretroviral, pediatric, perinatal, and opportunistic-infection guidance by filename and title.
- Preserve panel attribution and the edition or update date recorded in each document.

## Retrieval Use

- Retrieve the section matching age group, pregnancy or perinatal context, and treatment or infection topic.
- Keep recommendation qualifiers, drug tables, and section-specific update context together.
- Use cited source sections rather than mixing recommendations across population-specific volumes.

## Source Handling

- Preserve the recorded public-domain source metadata.
- Treat the bundled edition as a snapshot without claiming live update verification.
