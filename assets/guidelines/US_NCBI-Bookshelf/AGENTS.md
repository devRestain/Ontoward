# NCBI Bookshelf Reports and Guidance Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `bookshelf_*.md` as book and report material hosted by NCBI Bookshelf.
- Distinguish systematic evidence reviews, clinical guidance, and other report chapters by catalog type.
- Use the organization field to identify authorship such as AHRQ, VA, university, or institutional publishers.

## Retrieval Use

- Retrieve clinical findings with the review methods, population, and evidence date in view.
- Separate appendices, search strategies, extraction forms, and front matter from clinical conclusions.
- Treat Bookshelf as a hosting platform rather than one author or uniform license.

## Source Handling

- Inspect the individual public-domain or Creative Commons condition.
- Preserve NBK identifiers and original section context in provenance.
