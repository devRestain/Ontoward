# NCI PDQ Cancer Information Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `nci_pdq_*.md` as converted NCI PDQ cancer information and evidence summaries.
- Distinguish health-professional evidence reviews from patient-facing descriptions.
- Use titles and catalog types to identify treatment, screening, prevention, supportive-care, or genetic-risk context.

## Retrieval Use

- Retrieve passages matching cancer type, stage, population, and the document's intended audience.
- Preserve evidence-summary context rather than treating every passage as a formal practice recommendation.
- Keep textual figure references separate from unavailable original images.

## Source Handling

- Retain the recorded public-domain metadata and PDQ editorial-board attribution.
- Treat source dates as the bundled snapshot rather than proof of current cancer guidance.
