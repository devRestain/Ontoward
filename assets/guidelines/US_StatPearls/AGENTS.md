# StatPearls Clinical Overviews Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `statpearls_*.md` as textbook-style disease, procedure, and nursing overviews.
- Distinguish clinician chapters, nursing chapters, and archived chapters through titles and source URLs.
- Use the catalog's textbook-overview role rather than labeling these chapters as formal clinical guidelines.

## Retrieval Use

- Use symptom, examination, differential, and evaluation sections as supporting clinical background.
- Preserve chapter date, audience, and section context with retrieved evidence.
- Keep archived material explicit when considering its relevance.

## Source Handling

- Inspect each chapter's recorded license instead of assuming the dominant CC BY-NC-ND label covers every file.
- Keep any source-modification distribution restriction attached to downstream preparation choices.
