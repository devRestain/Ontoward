# USPSTF Preventive Recommendations Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `uspstf_*.md` as converted preventive screening and counseling recommendation statements.
- Read recommendation grades together with eligible populations, risk factors, and qualification text.
- Distinguish final statements, older recommendations, and other status information from the document body.

## Retrieval Use

- Use these statements for preventive screening or counseling plans matching the stated population.
- Keep preventive eligibility separate from diagnostic workup of a symptomatic presentation.
- Preserve the grade and population together when preparing cited search passages.

## Source Handling

- Retain the recorded USPSTF copyright notice and attribution conditions.
- Avoid presenting a source snapshot as a verified current recommendation.
