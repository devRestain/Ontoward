# VA and DoD Clinical Guidance Contract

## Content Meaning

- Treat `vadod_*.md` as converted VA and DoD guideline-related PDFs.
- Distinguish full guidelines, quick-reference guides, provider summaries, patient summaries, tools, and supplemental material.
- Use the title and catalog type to identify the clinical topic, audience, and edition.

## Retrieval Use

- Use full guidelines for recommendation context and quick references for navigation to that context.
- Use patient summaries for explanation context rather than clinician recommendation grading.
- Preserve algorithm branches, qualifying statements, and clinical population when selecting a passage.

## Source Handling

- Retain the recorded public-domain metadata and VA or DoD attribution.
- Inspect PDF conversion before interpreting algorithms or multi-column tables.
