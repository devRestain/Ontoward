59: IBD patients presenting to the clinic require a thorough evaluation for signs and symptoms of EIMs. Common presenting complaints include abdominal pain and bloating; diarrhea/constipation; fever; fatigue; weight loss; tenesmus; and inflammation of the joints, skin, eyes, and liver. A thorough family history of IBD is essential, as having a family member with IBD increases the likelihood of having the disease.
